# The clip

## Source

Rockstar Games' official "Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2" on YouTube
(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQRLujxTm3c), downloaded in this session
with yt-dlp. The trailer was captured entirely in-game on PlayStation 5.
No other footage is used; nothing is re-ordered or mixed in.

## The moment

One continuous segment, trailer time 117.0s → 142.0s (25 s):

- 117s — a police car explodes on a highway overpass. The trailer's
  biggest single beat, and the hook: it lands inside the first 2 seconds,
  the moment a Shorts viewer decides to stay or swipe.
- 119–123s — the crash aftermath, a night drive, Jason and Lucia together.
- 125–127s — Vice City street colour and neon: the place itself is the
  second hook, the city everyone waited 13 years to see again.
- 129–137s — a comic breather (a gun-shop ad on a living-room TV).
- 139–142s — the climb back to action: a boat and helicopter over water,
  then the motorcycle riding under a low plane — the trailer's most
  iconic image — to close the footage.

Why this stretch and not the opening: the trailer's first 40 seconds are
character setup and dialogue, slow for a vertical feed. The 117–142 run is
the trailer's own action climax, and it opens with an explosion, which is
what a Short needs in frame one.
